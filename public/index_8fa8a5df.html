Greetings, Peace and Health:

First and foremost, I view myself as a “helper”, a guide a coach. I believe we all have the ability and resources to resolve the challenges of daily living. I perceive struggle as an opportunity to grow. Those of us, who enjoy life abundantly, understand this universal law. The reality is- life can be overwhelming, contributing to stress which can cause distorted thinking and cognition. Of which, the consequences being, a lifetime of emotional pain! As a professional helper, my passion is to help the individual become Self-actualized, e.g., maximize their individual potential! Each of us is born with unique natural gifts. However, the true measure of a life spent well, is what we do with the talents that we were given. The human spirit desires acknowledgement and validation! I take great pride and satisfaction in helping others navigate the maze of 21st century life. My twenty years of helping, has taught me that- “We, is more powerful than me”, and “I am my brother’s keeper!”

I am fully licensed and experienced working with adults and adolescents; skilled treating clinical issues such as Depression, Anxiety, and Marital Discord, Parenting issues, Grief/Loss, Substance Abuse and Dependence. I’m a National Board Certified Counselor (nbcc), Licensed Professional Counselor of Mental Health (LPCMH), and International Certified Alcohol & Drug Counselor (ICADC). I look forward to our meeting.
